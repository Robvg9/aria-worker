# BLOCK 10 — ARIA RESOURCE / COST INTELLIGENCE
Status: CERTIFIED V1

Objective: evolve routing into a governed economic decision layer that chooses a sufficiently good resource with lower combined cost, latency and risk while preserving deterministic behavior and execution boundaries.

Missions:
10.1 Economic Decision Contract
10.2 Complexity & Token Estimation
10.3 Cost / Latency / Compute Modeling
10.4 Risk & Expected-Value Gating
10.5 Budget / Capacity Constraints
10.6 Economic Router Overlay
10.7 Deterministic Selection & Evidence
10.8 Regression + LIVE Certification

Safety boundaries:
- Pure decision layer; no provider calls or inference.
- No secret or credential access.
- Classic Router contract remains backward compatible.
- Unknown economic dimensions remain explicit; they are never coerced to zero or invented.
- Execution authority remains outside Resource Intelligence and must be re-checked by downstream governance.
